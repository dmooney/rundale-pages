Live call 1: Mícheál greeted at the ridges

Prompt-lab case e-h10-micheal-ridges-remark. Request built from the lab's request template and content files, sent to /v1/endpoints/limerick-demo/rundale-dialogue/versions/1 with a Firebase anonymous identity and App Check (temporary debug token, deleted afterwards). HTTP 200 in 7.7 s (first call, cold).

Connolly Cottage, Monday 27 March 1820, 06:45, light rain. Mícheál (38, smallholder and drover, wary) is working the potato ridges behind the cottage. Nobody else is here. No earlier talk.

Stranger: Hard work, that.

Mícheál: It is that, and the ground's no friend to it this week with the wet. Are you from these parts yourself, or did you come up by the Dublin mail?

He agrees, adds his own grumble about the wet, and hands the stranger a question. One flaw to judge: "the Dublin mail" is a detail the request does not give him. This is the invention risk the PR describes.

Live call 2: Mícheál, surly, at the ridges

Prompt-lab case e-h13-micheal-surly-ridges. HTTP 200 in 1.5 s.

Same place and hour.

Stranger: Any chance of a word, friend? I'd like to hear how the farm is run.

Mícheál: The ground's too heavy this morning for much talk, and the potatoes won't wait on a stranger's curiosity. If you've business in the parish, you'll find Peig Hannigan down the lane at the Letter Office, but if it's farming you're after knowing, you'd better tell me plain what you're at before I take another spadeful.

A busy, wary man who refuses the long talk but gives the stranger something to take up: a grievance, a person to see, and a challenge to say what they want. "Down the lane" is a direction the request may not give; the held-out numbers below count this kind of slip.

Before, for the same kind of moment

The frozen prompt's replies, from the lab's calibration sheet (promptfoo/prompt-lab/calibration-2026-11-24.md), different cases, same character and model:

Stranger: They tell me you drive cattle. Is it hard going, and do you go far with them?

Mícheál (frozen): The ground's soft enough to break a baste's leg, and as far as Athlone if the droving's good.

Stranger: There is. Tell me, what kind of a place is Kilteevan to live in?

Mícheál (frozen): It's a place where a man minds his own ditch.

Each is a final answer with no way in for the player. This is the terseness the owner found on the iPhone.
