PR 2125: ask which Connolly

iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.5), live against limerick-prod (rundale-intent v1, rundale-dialogue v1). Not a physical iPhone.

  1. Go to Connolly Cottage; Mícheál and Róisín are home.
  2. Type "ask Connolly about the household": the game asks "Which Connolly do you mean?" with two choices. No one answers, and there is no busy indicator or Stop.
  3. Choose the young woman (Róisín): a "Directed to…" receipt, then her live reply.
