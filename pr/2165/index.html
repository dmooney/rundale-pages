Asking for someone elsewhere answers "not here"

PR #2165, part of #2047 (spec Milestone 3: the player cannot converse normally with someone who is not available).

iPhone SE (3rd generation) simulator, iOS 26.5, running the shared engine from this branch. Not a physical-device result. UI test RundalePhase3ClarificationUITests.testAskingForSomeoneElsewhereSaysTheyAreNotHere, recorded with record-ui-test.py and RUNDALE_DEMO_HOLD=3; trimmed to the app, final state held 5 s.

What it shows

  1. A new game in Kilteevan Village. /wait 60 brings the clock to 08:00, and /people shows Peig, the letter-office keeper, waiting on the road. Mícheál is at Connolly Cottage.
  2. "ask Mícheál about the cattle" gets Mícheál is not here.
  3. "Mícheál, how are the cattle?" also gets Mícheál is not here. Peig does not answer for him, and no dialogue call is made.
